Model scope, numerical setup & publication notes

How the components are coupled

A bounded second-order Lagrangian reconstruction supplies the choppy surface. Its parent-wave dynamics couple to a three-dimensional Craik–Leibovich mean-flow model in Oceananigans.

The Cho disturbance feels the parent boundary forcing, parent orbital velocities, and the surface mean current. Cho does not yet feed momentum or energy back into the parent or current, so underwater changes cannot be attributed to capillary feedback.

This is a reduced wave / mean-flow model. It reconstructs a moving surface separately from a flat mean tank top; it does not resolve the moving air–water interface.
