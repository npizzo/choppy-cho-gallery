Interpretation

Comparing this replay with the 2.4× reference isolates the effect of Cho dissipation on the same forcing and transport histories. Lower damping need not produce a monotonic increase in every slope statistic: the capillary-band budget separates short-wave roughness from signed parent–Cho slope interference. The parent remains a retained-order model, and a large Cho correction can exceed the perturbative coupling assumption. These results are not a turbulence-resolution or experimental-onset convergence study.
