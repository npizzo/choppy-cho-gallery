Forcing, interpretation, and validation
  • The friction velocity specifies current-delivered, effective water-side stress: τc = ρwateru*,c². Changing it alters the mean-flow forcing and the Craik–Leibovich wave/current interaction. It is not identified with the total atmospheric stress.
  • Cho feels the resulting parent-wave field and mean-current transport. This stress pathway is indirect: no direct aerodynamic growth or pressure forcing is added to either the parent waves or Cho.
  • A modulation fit and a Doppler prediction are separate tests. Matching the fitted backscatter alone does not establish the kinematic prediction.
  • Queued and running cases contain no inferred result. Preliminary analyses are labelled explicitly; completed analyses require recorded passing checks and inspectable outputs.
  • Numerical completion is separate from physical validity. The scattering approximation, finite footprint, time sampling, and steep-slope limitations remain part of each case's interpretation.
  • Apparent radar velocity can include wave-motion bias. It is not automatically the model's Eulerian current or Stokes drift. An absolute U10 geophysical model function is not calibrated here.
