Operator comparison

Configured current operators: centered fourth-order advection and fourth-order accurate horizontal molecular diffusion. Molecular diffusion acts on the Eulerian current UE = UL − Pp; the surface stress treatment is retained. The stretched vertical grid and boundary diffusion remain second order. These changes do not make the entire coupled solver fourth-order accurate.

Benchmark and accuracy diagnostics

Higher-order diffusion improves the horizontal stencil; it is not a biharmonic viscosity or a change in the molecular viscosity. The Cho stage is separate, so a faster current step does not imply the same speedup for the complete calculation.

Interpretation

This is a coarse-current wave-response experiment. Short stability and operator checks do not establish turbulence-resolving DNS accuracy or long-time convergence.
