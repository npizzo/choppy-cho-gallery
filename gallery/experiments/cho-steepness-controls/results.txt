PHASE CONSISTENCY
The recent coupled campaign uses linear intrinsic parent phases. In the
controlled sweep this leaves a leading cubic resonant residual at the parent
wavelength, causing Cho to make a substantial long-wave adjustment. The
known isolated GC Stokes frequency shift cancels that cubic fundamental
residual; direct small-amplitude evaluation confirms fifth-order scaling
after the correction. It is not a source filter or amplitude calibration.

With linear phases, late total/parent MSS ratios are:
  ka=0.12: 0.87567
  ka=0.24: 0.64264
  ka=0.32: 0.53444
  ka=0.40: 0.54758

This demonstrates a material problem with treating the linear-phase coupled
parent response as a pure capillary roughness prediction. It does not imply
that every negative total-minus-parent MSS is an arithmetic error. All MSS
values are positive and the signed cross contribution closes the budget.

INTERPRETATION LIMITS
The corrected phase is formally consistent at retained order and removes the
leading resonant defect. It does not restore bounded cubic potential and
surface geometry; those can alter off-resonant forcing at the same cubic
order. The coupled model should retain consistent nonlinear parent dynamics
before using a steeper two-way run for quantitative roughness predictions.
At ka=.32, maximum |curvature*correction| is 0.052; at ka=.40 it is 0.190.
The parent maps remain invertible. Short-wave slope alone is not used as a
failure criterion, and neither slopes nor forcing were clipped or amplified.
