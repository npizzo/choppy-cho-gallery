Public output contract
======================

scripts/export_campaign.py reads a simulation bundle without importing or changing
its code. Keep this repository and the export destination outside that bundle;
validation fingerprints therefore remain unchanged. Run with Python 3.11 or newer:

  python3 scripts/export_campaign.py --campaign BUNDLE --destination gallery
  python3 scripts/check_gallery.py gallery

Only data/pilot and data/prescribed are exported. Local smoke tests are not
substituted for Unity experiment results. The manifest is gallery/data/campaign.json,
schema version 1. Its top-level fields are title, updated_at, status, status_text,
validation, experiment, notes, and cases. Case fields are id, title, description,
status, status_text, tank_complete, observed_time_s, metrics, videos, figures,
downloads, and notices. Video, figure, and download src fields are relative to the
gallery root. Poster and physical timing fields are optional. Physical playback
speed is physical seconds per playback second. Underwater playback speed is not
inferred from its nonuniform saved frames; its physical interval is provided.

Status is a statement about synchronized outputs, not a scheduler report:
- awaiting_sync: no outputs are available for this case.
- partial: at least some records or products are available.
- outputs_available: a completed parent/current record, a surface movie, an
  underwater movie, and a consistent mean-square-slope table are available.
  This does not assert that every requested render succeeded or the job finished.

metadata.toml complete=true certifies only the parent/current stage. It does not
certify completion of Cho evolution or movie rendering. A passed.json record is
reported as record_available: the exporter does not rerun the source-fingerprint
validation. Absence of a result is never reported as a failed or running job.

Movies are copied only when their renderer JSON completion record exists. The
renderer metadata is allowlisted: absolute input paths and arbitrary descriptions
are never copied. Movie files that are still changing or at least 90 MiB are
omitted with an explanatory notice. Existing PNG posters are copied; no poster is
invented for an underwater movie. Content-addressed filenames and atomic manifest
replacement protect the previously published snapshot. Old assets remain safe
until deliberately pruned, so the checker also enforces an 800 MiB gallery limit.

Diagnostic tables are parsed, checked, and rewritten as numeric CSV. The exporter
checks finite values, increasing time, nonnegative parent/Cho/total MSS, and
total_MSS = parent_MSS + Cho_MSS + cross_contribution. The cross contribution is
signed; it is not itself a mean-square slope. Sideband elevation variance is the
sum of squared complex free-mode amplitudes outside the largest initial mode,
divided by their total. Bound harmonics and Cho roughness are excluded. This is
not a wave-energy fraction. Matplotlib is optional: without it the numeric CSV
and summary metrics remain available, with an explicit missing-chart notice.

Inputs are limited to known metadata names, the two diagnostic tables, and four
rendered movie names with their optional PNG posters. Symlinks are rejected. Raw
NPZ, binary fields, checkpoints, local configuration paths, job logs, and SSH
credentials are never exported. The checker rejects unexpected file types,
unsafe references, absent assets, private host paths, common credential forms,
nonfinite metrics, unsupported schemas, and inconsistent status claims.

The public metrics are numerical consistency diagnostics, not proof of physical
convergence or saturation. Cho does not feed back on the parent or mean current;
underwater changes must not be described as caused by Cho roughness.
